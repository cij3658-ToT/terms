Building something meaningful together

To build a thriving team or community, it’s essential to have some guidelines, so that everyone knows how they are expected to show up. These are our terms and conditions for the Teams of Tomorrow® portal.

The Teams of Tomorrow® portal offers safe access to our community of practice — a regenerative space where we learn, collaborate, and transform together. These guidelines help us create the conditions for genuine transformation through interdependent learning and complexity-aware practice.

1.  Help to create a safe and inclusive environment

Our community is a curated space where all members can explore, unlearn, and transform. Everyone should feel welcome and valued as they navigate the discomfort that often precedes genuine growth. We do not tolerate discriminatory or harassing behaviour, including cyberbullying, hate speech, or targeted harassment — whether directed at members or others. We uphold psychological safety while honouring that transformation requires us to sit with uncertainty and challenge our current ways of thinking. Be respectful of those sitting in this discomfort and encourage them as they explore what they don’t yet know, supporting each other to out-think current situations rather than retreat to familiar patterns.

2.  Please engage professionally and respectfully

This community thrives through interdependent learning — where we evolve our thinking together rather than defending fixed positions. Interactions should be respectful and curious, embracing healthy dialogue and differing viewpoints while avoiding personal attacks, inflammatory remarks, or dismissive behaviour. When engaging, practice suspend, respect, listen, voice — allowing collective wisdom and emergent insight to develop. Remember that we’re working in tune with life’s flow, where transformation happens through relationship and co-creation, not through imported solutions or expert-driven answers.

3.  No unsolicited promotions or spam

This space is dedicated to genuine learning and transformation, not commercial promotion. Posts identified as self-promotion, advertisements, or irrelevant solicitations may be removed at our discretion, as they distract from our regenerative purpose and the emergent potential of our collective exploration.

4.  Keep discussions relevant

Our community is dedicated to transformational learning that serves not only human stakeholders but the ecosystems that support us. We don’t promote alignment to any single philosophy, model, or framework. Instead, we encourage dialogue over debate — sharing ideas, thoughts, and proposals as we wonder together, think out loud, and respond to emergent challenges with complexity awareness. We’re here to learn how to learn, which requires openness to vertical development: genuine change in our thoughts, emotions, and actions.

5.  Respect privacy and confidentiality

This is a private learning space for Teams of Tomorrow® collaborators and members. Respect each other’s privacy by not sharing personal or confidential information without consent. Trust is essential for the kind of vulnerable learning and unlearning we’re here to practice together.

Violations of these guidelines will be addressed as follows

Content that violates these standards may be removed without notice.

Repeated violations may result in removal from the community.

By participating in this community, you agree to uphold these guidelines and contribute to a regenerative learning environment where transformation becomes possible. If you have concerns or need clarification, please contact Teams of Tomorrow®.
